Federation and duplicate boundary

External records enter a normalized research-lead queue, not the published count. Promotion requires a stable origin ID, source review, evidence and confidence states, facts, significance, goalpost / response, Maybe / Therefore, consequences, revision provenance, and a duplicate/lifecycle decision. Matching records are crosslinked to one canonical event rather than copied. Counts from different comparability groups are never summed.

Capabilities being brought home

Each feature keeps its own evidence and lifecycle controls.

Adopt from Trump Archive

Canonical Statement Artifact Links

Keep raw posts, speeches, and documents in a separate artifact layer, then link them to verified events rather than counting each utterance as an event.

Adopt from UndoTrump

Policy Lifecycle And Restoration States

Add proposed, implemented, in litigation, partially blocked, reversed, expired, restored, and outcome-measured states with dated transitions.

Adopt from Immigration Policy Tracking Project

Predecessor Subsequent Action And Litigation Chains

Connect a policy to the rule or directive it replaced, later implementation, litigation, court actions, rescission, replacement, and continuing effect.

Adopt from Record47

Evidence Confidence Review And Revision Controls

Add normalized evidence tiers, confidence and dispute states, human-review state, source preservation, official responses, contradiction arcs, consequence chains, and revision history.

Adopt from The Record

Complete Federated Interpretive Record

Treat external archives as discovery and provenance inputs. Every record that advances beyond research lead must be rewritten from inspected sources into facts, significance, goalpost/response, maybe_therefore, evidence/confidence, provenance, consequences, and revisions, with no copied bulk prose.

Adopt from All four archives

Federated Search

Search The Record first and show separately labeled external results by unit. A public-statement result, policy action, immigration policy, and executive-conduct entry must retain different badges and never collapse into an undifferentiated count.

Preservation rule

data/legacy_entries.json is now the canonical historical body. The root and docs/ HTML custody representations retain active rows and superseded tombstones so retired permalinks keep working; the generated entries_array.js compatibility view contains active rows only so downstream consumers cannot double-count retired records. Companion DOCX/PDF files remain dated snapshots until their separate rebuild path is restored.
