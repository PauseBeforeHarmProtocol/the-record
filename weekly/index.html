  • The proposal would end the Schedule A administrative exemption for professional nurses, physical therapists and people of exceptional ability in sciences or arts, requiring those occupations to use the general PERM labor-market test. If finalized, the proposal would change how employers across health care, technology and other sectors demonstrate the absence of available U.S. workers before employment-based permanent residence. Its reach is national, while the actual legal and labor-market effects remain unresolved.
